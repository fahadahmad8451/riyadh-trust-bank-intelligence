# HR Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This HR Leave Policy explains the leave that employees of Riyadh Trust Bank are entitled to, how to request leave, and who approves it. It applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their working hours. This policy follows the Saudi Labour Law, and if the law gives the employee more leave than this policy, the law applies.

## 2. Annual Leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to 30 calendar days of paid annual leave per year, after completing the probation period of three months. Annual leave is counted in calendar days, including weekends that fall inside the leave period. Employees may take their leave in one block or in parts, but no single block may be shorter than five days unless the manager agrees. Leave must be taken within the year it is earned; up to 10 unused days may be carried to the next year, and any further unused days are lost unless the manager has refused the leave in writing.

## 3. How to Request Leave

The employee must submit the leave request through the HR system at least 14 days before the leave starts, and at least 30 days before for leave longer than 15 days. The request must state the start date, the end date and the number of days. The direct manager must approve or reject the request within three working days. If the manager does not reply within three working days, the employee should remind them; silence is not approval. Leave is only confirmed once it is approved in the system.

## 4. Sick Leave

An employee who is ill is entitled to paid sick leave of up to 30 days per year, on presentation of a medical certificate from a recognised hospital or clinic. The first 30 days are paid in full, the next 60 days at three quarters of the salary, and any further sick leave in the same year is unpaid. The employee must inform their manager on the first day of sickness and must send the medical certificate within three days of returning to work.

## 5. Other Leave Types

Maternity leave is 10 weeks of paid leave, taken from four weeks before the expected delivery date. Paternity leave is 3 days of paid leave on the birth of a child. Marriage leave is 5 days of paid leave. Bereavement leave is 5 days of paid leave on the death of a spouse, parent or child, and 3 days for other close relatives. Hajj leave is 10 to 15 days of paid leave once during employment, for employees who have completed two years of service. Emergency leave of up to 3 days may be granted by the manager for urgent family reasons.

## 6. Public Holidays

The bank is closed on official public holidays announced by the government, including Eid Al-Fitr, Eid Al-Adha and National Day. If a public holiday falls during an employee's annual leave, that day is not counted as annual leave.

## 7. Unpaid Leave and Abuse

Unpaid leave may be granted by the HR Director for up to 30 days in special cases, and it does not count towards the annual leave balance. Taking leave without approval is treated as unauthorised absence and may lead to a salary deduction and disciplinary action. Forging a medical certificate is gross misconduct and leads to dismissal.
