# Account Opening Policy

## 1. Purpose

This Account Opening Policy explains how a new account is opened at Riyadh Trust Bank, which types of accounts exist, and what the customer and the branch officer must do at each step. The policy works together with the KYC Policy, which explains how identity is verified. No account may be opened without a completed KYC check.

## 2. Types of Accounts

The bank offers three main types of personal accounts. A current account is for daily transactions, salary and payments, and comes with a debit card and online banking. A savings account earns a small profit on the balance and is for money the customer wants to keep aside. A fixed deposit account locks the money for a chosen term, from 3 months to 24 months, and earns a higher profit, but the money cannot be withdrawn early without a penalty. The customer may hold one account of each type.

## 3. Documents Needed for Account Opening

What documents are needed for KYC account opening? The customer must bring a valid National ID card for Saudi nationals, or a valid Iqama for residents, as the identity document. The customer must also bring proof of address dated within the last three months, such as a utility bill or a national address certificate. For a salary account, an employment letter or salary certificate is also needed. For a minor's account, the guardian must bring the minor's birth certificate and their own identity document. All originals must be seen; photocopies alone are not accepted.

## 4. Steps to Open an Account

First, the customer fills in the account opening form, either in the branch or online. Second, the branch officer checks the identity document and the proof of address against the KYC Policy. Third, the officer enters the customer details into the core banking system and runs the sanctions and watchlist check. Fourth, the customer signs the account terms and conditions and chooses the account type. Fifth, the account number is created and the debit card is ordered. The whole process in the branch should take no more than 45 minutes if all documents are ready. Online applications are reviewed within one working day.

## 5. Accounts for Minors and Joint Accounts

An account for a minor under 18 years may only be opened by a parent or legal guardian, who operates the account until the minor turns 18. A joint account may be opened by two people, who must both be present, both pass the KYC check, and both sign the form. Either holder may operate the joint account unless the form says both signatures are needed.

## 6. Account Activation and Cards

The account becomes active as soon as the account number is created, but the debit card is sent to the branch within five working days. The customer must activate the card at an ATM or through the mobile app with the PIN sent by SMS. Online banking is activated at the same time, after the customer sets a password and a one-time code is verified.

## 7. Closing an Account

A customer may close an account at any time by visiting a branch with their identity document. The remaining balance is paid to the customer after any outstanding fees are deducted. The bank may close an account if it has had no activity for 12 months and the balance is zero, after sending two written notices to the customer. All account records are kept for ten years after closure, as required by the KYC Policy.
