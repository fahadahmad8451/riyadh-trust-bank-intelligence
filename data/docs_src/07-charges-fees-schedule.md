# Charges and Fees Schedule

## 1. Purpose

This Charges and Fees Schedule lists the fees that Riyadh Trust Bank charges for its services. All fees are in Saudi Riyals (SAR) and include VAT where VAT applies. The bank must show this schedule to the customer before the customer signs for a product, and the schedule on the bank's website is always the current one. If this document and the website disagree, the website version applies and the customer must be told.

## 2. Account Fees

Opening a current account or a savings account is free. There is no monthly fee for a current account as long as the average monthly balance is SAR 1,000 or more. If the average balance falls below SAR 1,000, a monthly fee of SAR 25 applies. There is no fee for a savings account. A fixed deposit account has no opening fee and no monthly fee. Closing an account is free.

## 3. Card Fees

The first debit card is free. A replacement debit card costs SAR 30 if the card is lost or damaged by the customer, and is free if the card is faulty. A credit card has an annual fee of SAR 250 for the standard card and SAR 500 for the gold card, waived in the first year. A supplementary credit card costs SAR 100 per year. A PIN replacement at the branch costs SAR 15.

## 4. Transfer Fees

Transfers between Riyadh Trust Bank accounts are free. Transfers to another bank inside Saudi Arabia through SARIE cost SAR 7 for same-day transfer and are free for next-day transfer. International transfers cost SAR 50, plus any charges of the receiving bank. A transfer that is returned because the details were wrong costs SAR 25.

## 5. Cash and Cheque Fees

Cash withdrawals from the bank's own ATMs are free. Withdrawals from another bank's ATM in Saudi Arabia cost SAR 5 per withdrawal after the first four free withdrawals each month. A cheque book of 25 leaves costs SAR 25. A stopped cheque costs SAR 50. A returned cheque because of insufficient funds costs SAR 100 and is reported to the credit bureau.

## 6. Loan and Other Fees

The loan administrative fee is 1 percent of the loan amount, with a maximum of SAR 5,000, as set in the Personal Loan Policy. A late loan instalment fee is SAR 50 if the payment is more than 7 days late. A copy of a bank statement costs SAR 10 per page at the branch and is free through online banking. A liability letter or account certificate costs SAR 50.

## 7. Changes to Fees

The bank may change these fees with 30 days' notice, published on the website and sent to customers by SMS. No fee may be charged that is not listed in this schedule or in the product terms the customer signed. Any customer who believes they were charged wrongly may complain, and the complaint must be answered within 15 working days, as required by the SAMA Compliance Summary.
